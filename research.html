ChemSphere
Quantum Reaction Database
🚀 IN DEVELOPMENT

A quantum-native graph database of chemical reactions. ChemSphere encodes molecular structures and reaction pathways as Hamiltonian-coupled networks — enabling quantum chemistry profiling of reaction intermediates, transition states, and catalytic mechanisms. Reaction fingerprints are searchable and comparable across chemical space, providing a living atlas of quantum chemistry knowledge.

Graph Database Quantum Chemistry Reaction Profiling Chemical Space

HAHD
Hierarchical Agentic Hamiltonian Design
🚀 IN DEVELOPMENT

A multi-agent framework for building composite Hamiltonians for complex molecular and material systems. HAHD decomposes a target system into interacting sub-Hamiltonians — electronic, vibrational, spin, and environmental layers — each managed by a specialist AI agent. Enables quantum simulation of material synthesis pathways, drug-receptor interactions, and catalytic cycles at scales previously inaccessible.

Hamiltonian Design Multi-agent AI Material Synthesis Quantum Simulation

Inverse Design & Route Planning

An agentic research tool that designs synthesis routes for target materials using HAHD-generated Hamiltonians and perturbation inversion. Given a target property profile, the planner identifies which precursors, conditions, and reaction sequences are most likely to yield the desired structure — validated against ChemSphere's reaction database and ranked by confidence score.

Beyond ICD Codes

Dynamic Brain Health Record

The brain-specific instantiation of Q-EHR. Q-NEHR tracks cognitive state longitudinally, supports closed-loop neuromodulation, and provides quantitative biomarkers for mental health conditions far beyond symptom checklists.
