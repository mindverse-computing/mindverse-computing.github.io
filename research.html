Physics-informed modeling

Hybrid model development.
Evaluated scenario exploration.

Across Quantum Matter, Plasma, and Fluids, our research direction combines classical–quantum HPC with physics-informed surrogate modeling.

  1. 01 / COMPUTE

    Classical–quantum HPC

    Co-design classical simulation and quantum workflows for the physical problem, using CPU, GPU, and quantum computing resources in the research pipeline.

  2. 02 / MODEL

    PhysicsNeMo & PINNs

    Investigate physics-informed neural networks, neural operators, and surrogate architectures using physical equations and reference data.

  3. 03 / EVALUATE

    Surrogate development

    Train and compare models over a defined set of systems and operating conditions, with explicit accuracy and applicability measures.

  4. 04 / EXPLORE

    Scenario inference

    Use an evaluated surrogate to compare scenarios without repeating the full reference-model development workflow for every query.

The rapid scenario step is surrogate inference. The role and benefit of each classical or quantum component are evaluated within the research workflow.

Shared evaluation practice

Every result needs
a domain-specific reference.

Research claims should be supported by a defined task, documented methods, and a reproducible comparison.

01

Define the question

Specify data, task, encoding, assumptions, and what a useful result would mean.

02

Compare with classical methods

Match training data, constraints, and evaluation splits. Account for preprocessing, simulation, and hardware overhead.

03

Measure what matters

Record accuracy, uncertainty, robustness, latency, and resource use. Distinguish simulator results from hardware experiments.

04

Document applicability

Report failure cases, noise sensitivity, data limitations, and the conditions under which conclusions hold.

Research context

Clear assumptions.
Useful conversations.

What does “quantum” mean in these models?

It refers to the computational representation and methods being investigated. A quantum encoding of market, cellular, or neural data does not establish that those systems exhibit exploitable quantum coherence.

Is a quantum advantage established?

No quantum speedup or predictive advantage is claimed here. Each proposed workflow needs comparison with suitable classical methods and an accounting of total computational cost.
