QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

Quantum Virtual Finance — Research

QVF

Our finance research develops next-generation tools for understanding how markets move, how risk concentrates, and how portfolios can be managed with greater intelligence. These tools are built on our QVF platform and target systematic trading, risk management, and market intelligence.

What We're Building

We are developing market connectivity mapping tools, automated trading signal systems, and portfolio optimisation methods that detect regime changes faster than conventional approaches — and a monthly market intelligence report for institutional investors.

Research Products

  • Market Connectivity Map Tracks how assets and market sectors are linked and how those connections shift over time. Powers early-warning signals for regime changes.
  • Portfolio Optimisation Engine Finds allocations that balance risk and return under stressed conditions that conventional models miss.
  • High-Frequency Signal System Detects micro-structural market transitions in real time and generates execution signals at the moment of change.
  • Monthly Market Intelligence Report A monthly quantitative briefing covering market connectivity, risk regimes, and sector dynamics. For institutional investors and systematic funds.

QUANTUM CHEMISTRY & MATERIALS SCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Matter — Research

QVMa

Our materials research builds simulation tools that predict how molecules and materials behave at the atomic level — going beyond what classical chemistry software can model. We are creating databases, design frameworks, and planning tools for chemistry, energy, and drug discovery.

What We're Building

We are building a searchable database of chemical reactions, a framework for designing complex molecular systems layer by layer, and an intelligent tool that proposes how to synthesise a target material from first principles.

Research Products

  • ChemSphere A searchable database of chemical reactions encoded as interconnected networks. Researchers can query reaction pathways, compare molecular fingerprints, and identify known routes to target molecules.
  • Intelligent Molecular Design (HAHD) A design framework that breaks complex molecular systems into layers — electronic, structural, environmental — and uses specialist agents to simulate each layer independently and then together.
  • Synthesis Planner An intelligent planning tool: given a target material or molecule, it proposes which precursors, conditions, and reaction steps are most likely to produce it — then ranks and scores each candidate route.

GENOMICS & CELL BIOLOGY

Quantum Virtual Omics — Research

QVO

Our omics research translates complex biological data — from genes and proteins to metabolites and clinical signals — into dynamic models of how cells and patients function. These tools support drug discovery, personalised medicine, and the design of smarter clinical trials.

What We're Building

We are building tools that model biological state from multi-omics data, identify the molecular signatures of disease, rank potential therapeutic interventions by their predicted recovery effect, and create a new kind of patient health record that goes beyond static diagnostic codes.

Research Products

  • Biological State Modelling Integrates data from genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics into a unified model of how a cell or patient is functioning — and how that state changes under disease or treatment.
  • Target Discovery Engine Identifies which molecular disruptions are driving a disease state and ranks potential interventions — small molecules, biologics, or gene-level edits — by how effectively they restore healthy function.
  • Quantum Health Record (Q-EHR) A new approach to patient records that captures the dynamic biological state of a patient — not just a diagnosis label — enabling quantitative tracking of disease progression and treatment response over time.
  • Cell State Atlas A reference library of biological states across tissues, disease conditions, and treatment contexts. Used for comparing patient data, predicting drug response, and identifying disease biomarkers.

BCI & NEUROSCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Mind — Research

QVM

Our neuroscience research builds tools for understanding brain state, detecting neurological conditions early, and designing targeted brain stimulation therapies. These tools are for researchers, clinicians, and companies building the next generation of brain-computer interfaces.

What We're Building

We are building a reference atlas of neurological conditions with their brain-state signatures, a new kind of neural health record, an autonomous closed-loop brain stimulation system, and a library of brain states for EEG-based decoding.

Research Products

  • Neurological Condition Atlas A reference library of 27 neurological and psychiatric conditions, each described by its brain-state signature. Enables early detection, comparison, and prediction of which intervention best restores healthy brain function.
  • Neural Health Record (Q-NEHR) A dynamic record of a patient's brain health state — not just a symptom checklist — that tracks cognitive changes over time and supports personalised therapy decisions.
  • Closed-Loop Brain Stimulation An autonomous six-step system that continuously monitors brain state, compares it against a healthy reference, selects the optimal stimulation approach, applies it, and measures the recovery response.
  • Brain State Library A catalogue of 25 distinct brain states — spanning sleep, focus, emotion, and neurological conditions — that can be used to classify EEG signals and design brain-computer interface decoders.
