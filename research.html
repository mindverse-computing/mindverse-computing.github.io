Proprietary QDT Engine

QDT Engine

Our core engine models open dynamic systems with data-driven state evolution and continuous feedback. This shared substrate supports BCI, omics, and finance twins while preserving stability, traceability, and adaptability across changing conditions.

Why Integrated Quantum-Classical?

Classical pipelines fit correlations but often lose dynamic regulatory structure. Our integrated stack preserves state continuity and system-level relationships so intervention planning can be guided by consistent system behavior rather than trial-and-error tuning.

Core Capabilities

  • System-state initialization and continuity tracking
  • Data-conditioned model learning from live signals
  • Adaptive control with transition diagnostics
  • State extraction and stability scoring
  • Regulatory-mode mapping across operating conditions
  • Multi-view diagnostics for system monitoring
  • Cross-domain validation and consistency checks
  • Noise-aware hybrid simulation and hardware portability

AI Orchestration Layer

AI Orchestrator

Our orchestration layer coordinates data agents, simulation agents, and validation agents to keep every twin synchronized with incoming evidence. It continuously tests whether each system remains in a healthy regulatory mode and escalates when state geometry degrades.
