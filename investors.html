Company & investment

Continual physics models.
One Agentome platform.

Mindverse is developing Agentome around structure-preserving Large Physics Models (LPMs). Continual personalization would keep individual models aligned with incoming BCI data; backend agents would operate the technical lifecycle, while frontend agents guide users in learning, focus, creative wellbeing, and research. These capabilities need separate evidence and partner evaluation.

The investment rationale

One continual model core.
Two agent layers.

The platform hypothesis is that one personalized model foundation can support multiple guided experiences while backend agents reduce manual technical work. Model accuracy, continual adaptation, operational reliability, and user benefit must each be evaluated.

Model structure

A family of personal LPMs

The LPM family combines neural-state tracking with exploration of future neural-field dynamics. Personal versions, explicit horizons, and retained evaluation reports connect the model family to continual digital twins.

Planned business model

Ingest. Fit. Serve.

The proposed commercial model has three parts: ingest and manage BCI recordings, fit and continually evaluate personal LPMs, and serve model outputs plus agent-supported experiences through partner software. Backend orchestration supports all three; these remain development and pricing concepts.

01 / Ingest

Data platform

Proposed paid data-preparation work: document recording quality, preprocessing, annotations, and session provenance. Subscription terms remain a planning assumption.

02 / Fit

Continual LPM personalization

Proposed training and continual-adaptation engagements that deliver accepted personal model versions and evaluation records. Per-model and enterprise pricing are options to assess with partners.

03 / Serve

Agentome integrations

Proposed interfaces for evaluated model outputs, operational records, and frontend support and guidance. No production API specification, service level, or usage pricing is published here.
