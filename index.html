The Agentome experience

Track the present.
Personalize over time.

Our Large Physics Models (LPMs) describe neural dynamics. Continual LPMs (cLPMs) are the planned personalized versions updated from incoming recordings, with evaluation before an update is accepted.

Backend agents / in development

Autonomous behind the scenes

Manage recording quality, data preparation, training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring within agreed settings. The aim is for users to take part without manually operating the modeling infrastructure.

Frontend agents / in development

Personalized guidance for users

Help users set goals, understand model-informed suggestions, choose sessions, and give feedback. Guidance spans learning, focus, meditation, music, art, and restoration; research workflows retain investigator review.

A precise modeling scope

A twin of recorded
dynamics.

We use “digital twin” to mean a fitted model of recorded dynamics, tied to its input recordings and model version. A comparison across sessions must account for recording quality, acquisition changes, and task context.

What the model represents

  • Dynamics fitted to a person’s recordings and model version
  • Changes relative to their own baseline
  • Named model parameters a researcher can inspect
  • Tracking and forecasting checked separately on held-out data

What that does not establish

  • A replica of a biological brain
  • A model of consciousness
  • A diagnostic or treatment device
  • A demonstrated quantum speedup

Development milestones

Advance when the evidence supports it.
