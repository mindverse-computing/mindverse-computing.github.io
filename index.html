Health Records

Quantum Neural EHR (Q-NEHR)

A new class of medical record built around the quantum digital twin. Instead of fragmented test scores, the Q-NEHR stores each patient's healthy baseline, the precise signature of their condition, and how it evolves over time. The result is a longitudinal, privacy-first record that physicians can query, compare across cohorts, and use to guide truly personalised treatment decisions.

  • Personal baseline of brain health
  • Precise signatures of neurological conditions
  • Longitudinal tracking across visits and cohorts
  • Privacy-preserving by design
  • Interoperable with existing EHR systems
