📈 Quantitative Finance

Quantum Virtual Finance

QVF

QVF maps market behavior into a continuously updated Market State Tensor to monitor regime changes and directional risk. Agentic workflows rank anomalies, generate regime hypotheses, and recommend portfolio triage faster than static factor pipelines.

How It Works

The market twin uses adaptive state evolution and multi-signal diagnostics to classify operating conditions and detect transitions. Its intelligent layer turns state shifts into auditable risk-control and portfolio-adjustment recommendations the moment market structure begins to change.

What It Does

  • Continuously monitors market state from live and historical signals
  • Detects regime shifts and structural transitions in real time
  • Surfaces risk anomalies with early-warning indicators
  • Generates an auditable market-state record over time
  • Runs consistency checks across multiple time horizons
  • Delivers agentic portfolio adjustment recommendations
  • Supports stress testing and resilience scenario analysis
  • Integrates with existing quantitative research infrastructure

⚒ Quantum Chemistry & Materials Science

Quantum Virtual Matter

QVMa

Molecular and materials systems are encoded as agentic quantum state models. QVMa constructs a Material State Tensor from electronic structure data, molecular geometries, and condensed matter configurations. Intelligent workflows turn simulated dynamics into experiment plans, candidate rankings, and hypothesis refinement beyond classical computational chemistry limits.

How It Works

QVMa combines quantum simulation of electronic structure with molecular dynamics and many-body physics to model materials at a level of accuracy beyond classical methods. Its intelligent orchestration layer supports materials discovery, catalysis design, and experiment prioritization.

What It Does

  • Builds quantum state models from molecular and materials data
  • Calculates ground-state energies for complex chemical structures
  • Classifies condensed matter phases and material properties
  • Simulates molecular dynamics with quantum-level accuracy
  • Predicts catalysis reaction pathways and energy barriers
  • Generates band structures for semiconductor and photovoltaic research
  • Ranks experimental candidates using intelligent workflows
  • Supports composite system design and experiment planning

🧬 Genomics & Omics

Quantum Virtual Omics

QVO

QVO represents patient-specific biology as a Cellular State Tensor that captures health and disease drift. This enables systematic comparison of baseline state, disrupted state, and candidate intervention response, with intelligent workflows ranking biological pathways and research next steps.

How It Works

QVO learns regulatory relationships from multi-omics inputs, tracks biological state displacement under perturbations, and evaluates recovery trajectories under candidate interventions. Built-in state records support longitudinal review, reproducibility, and research collaboration.

What It Does

  • Integrates multi-omics data into a unified biological state model
  • Identifies cell-phenotype states and disease-associated patterns
  • Fingerprints disease perturbations and dysregulation signatures
  • Generates longitudinal patient state records for research tracking
  • Classifies regulatory disruption types across biological pathways
  • Simulates tissue-scale state landscapes for disease modeling
  • Ranks intervention candidates by predicted state recovery
  • Supports drug discovery and combination therapy research

🧠 BCI & Neuroscience

Quantum Virtual Mind

QVM

QVM maps high-density brain signal streams into a subject-specific Mental State Tensor for continuous cognitive-state tracking. This supports dynamic interpretation of how the brain is functioning — rather than simple snapshot labels — and enables personalised cognitive monitoring over time.

How It Works

The BCI pipeline combines advanced neural signal encoding, adaptive connectivity estimation, real-time state modeling, and intelligent cognitive-state triage for robust cognitive tracking. It is designed for perturbation analysis, longitudinal monitoring, and closed-loop neuromodulation research.

What It Does

  • Encodes brain signals into a continuously updated cognitive state model
  • Estimates adaptive connectivity between brain regions in real time
  • Tracks subject-specific cognitive state changes over sessions
  • Detects stability loss, drift, and anomalies in neural dynamics
  • Diagnoses cognitive-state topology and functional network patterns
  • Stores longitudinal timelines for individual and cohort research
  • Classifies cognitive conditions using intelligent state-aware analysis
  • Designs closed-loop neuromodulation recommendations
