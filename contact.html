About Mindverse Computing

We develop the agentic quantum digital twin learned from BCI data — a full-stack platform that reconstructs an executable quantum-classical state model and routes it into agentic workflows. Our aim is to help research teams understand how learning and disorder move the dynamics a brain–computer interface can see.

Research and development notice

Our platforms are experimental research tools for academic collaboration and proof-of-concept study. We do not provide medical diagnoses, treatment, healthcare services, or any regulated service. All work is developed using public, synthetic, or anonymised research data.
