Research roadmap

Progress defined
by evidence.

Our research priorities connect domain-specific questions with traceable references, reproducible models, and focused evaluation.

01 / Fluid Dynamics ↓02 / Plasma Dynamics ↓03 / Quantum Matter ↓04 / Quantum Virtual Omics ↓05 / Quantum Virtual Mind ↓06 / Quantum Virtual Finance ↓

From reference to application

Define. Compare.
Evaluate. Refine.

Each research direction advances through demonstrable results. Methods, assumptions, and limitations are reviewed at every stage.

  1. 01 / REFERENCE

    Frame the problem

    Define a system, a bounded question, and a traceable dataset or reference calculation.

  2. 02 / WORKFLOW

    Build the comparison

    Prepare reproducible data, model, and compute workflows with appropriate classical baselines.

  3. 03 / EVIDENCE

    Evaluate the model

    Compare held-out behavior, physical or domain consistency, uncertainty, and computational cost.

  4. 04 / APPLICATION

    Scope a focused study

    Use the evidence to refine assumptions and define an application-specific research collaboration.

Quantum Virtual Mind

Quantum Virtual Finance

Research in development

Does a proposed state or optimization model add value under time-ordered, cost-aware evaluation?
