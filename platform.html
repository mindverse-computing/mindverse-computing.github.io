Fluid Dynamics

Research in development

Flow and thermal behavior shape the performance of engineered systems. We explore simulation and AI approaches for understanding fields, comparing configurations, and investigating design choices.

  • Fluid flow Velocity, pressure, forces, and geometry dependent behavior.
  • Thermal systems Heat transfer, cooling, and coupled flow and temperature studies.
  • Complex flows Research in turbulence, mixing, and multiphase systems.

Example applications

Aircraft aerodynamics ↗ Automotive aerodynamics ↗ High-speed & missile flows ↗

Starting inputs

Geometry, boundary conditions, fluid properties, and reference flow or thermal data.

Research objectives

Research toward field prediction, flow analysis, and design parameter exploration.

Evaluation approach

Comparison with reference solutions or measurements over a defined geometry and operating range.

02 / Physics AI

Plasma Dynamics

Research in development

Plasmas connect particle motion, electromagnetic fields, and complex transport. Our research direction brings physics modeling and AI to questions in plasma behavior and process engineering.

  • Plasma behavior Particle and field interactions, transport, and instability studies.
  • Process research Discharge conditions, deposition, etching, and surface interactions.
  • Confinement Modeling questions in magnetically confined plasma research.
Discuss a research question↗

Example applications

Tokamak plasma ↗ Pulsed FRC compression ↗ Plasma processing ↗

Starting inputs

Geometry, operating conditions, field configurations, and diagnostic or simulation data.

Research objectives

Research toward field prediction, transport analysis, and process response models.

Evaluation approach

Reference comparisons within a specified plasma regime, with physical assumptions and boundary conditions documented.

04 / Quantum AI

Quantum Virtual Omics

Research in development

Connect multiple biological views of a cell. Quantum Virtual Omics investigates cellular state representations built from genomics, proteomics, and metabolomics for computational biology and in-silico experimental design.

  • Multi-omics integration Connect genomic, protein, and metabolic measurements.
  • Cellular modeling Explore state changes, pathways, and perturbation responses.
  • In-silico research Compound screening, biomarker studies, and experimental prioritization.
Discuss QVO↗

Starting inputs

Genomics, proteomics, metabolomics, molecular features, and annotated research datasets.

Technical architecture

Multi-omics Hamiltonian construction, cellular state tensors, variational quantum methods, and tensor network compression.

Evaluation approach

Held-out biological datasets, classical model comparisons, uncertainty analysis, and independent experimental follow-up where appropriate.

Explore platform components +
  • Multi-omics Hamiltonian constructor
  • Cellular State Tensor for perturbation studies
  • Variational molecular modeling research with VQE / QAOA
  • Protein–ligand interaction modeling
  • ADMET research profiling workflows
  • Tensor network compression with DMRG / TEBD
  • Batch orchestration for in-silico experiments
  • Q-EHR integration research for longitudinal biological data

Research integration directions: Python workflows, cloud simulation APIs, on-premise HPC, classical tensor accelerators, and quantum hardware experiments.

Components and interfaces describe the research architecture; availability is discussed for each collaboration.

Example research

Cellular response models ↗ Compound screening ↗ Biomarkers & disease mechanisms ↗

05 / Quantum AI

Quantum Virtual Mind

Research in development

Investigate neural dynamics through a computational state model. Quantum Virtual Mind explores quantum–classical representations of neural research data for cognitive modeling, signal analysis, and experimental neurofeedback studies.

  • Neural state modeling Represent temporal dynamics and relationships in neural signals.
  • Cognitive research Study attention, arousal, sleep, and behavioral context.
  • Adaptive experiments Explore research neurofeedback and longitudinal model updates.
Discuss QVM↗

Starting inputs

EEG, fMRI, MEG, spiking data, biosensor measurements, and annotated neural research sessions.

Technical architecture

Neural Hamiltonian construction, MPS / PEPS tensor networks, multimodal signal fusion, and hybrid state-evolution experiments.

Evaluation approach

Held-out sessions and participants, classical decoding baselines, signal-quality checks, and explicit uncertainty and latency measurements.

Explore platform components +
  • Neural Hamiltonian constructor from research signals
  • Mental State Tensor with MPS / PEPS representations
  • Multimodal signal preprocessing and fusion
  • Quantum–classical state-evolution experiments
  • Cognitive-state classification research
  • HAHD closed-loop optimization research
  • Longitudinal model updating and personalization
  • Research APIs for offline or streaming experiments

Research integration directions: Python and on-device software experiments, cloud APIs, REST / WebSocket interfaces, and hybrid classical–quantum simulation.

Components and interfaces describe the research architecture; availability is discussed for each collaboration.

06 / Quantum Finance

Quantum Virtual Finance

Research in development

Explore financial markets as evolving systems. Quantum Virtual Finance investigates quantum representations of market state, regime transitions, portfolio constraints, and cross-asset relationships.

  • Market dynamics Regime detection, nonlinear correlations, and changing volatility.
  • Portfolio research Constrained allocation and risk–return trade-offs.
  • Risk scenarios Tail behavior, systemic exposure, and stress testing.
Discuss QVF↗

Starting inputs

Time-series prices, financial indicators, asset relationships, and defined portfolio constraints.

Technical architecture

Market Hamiltonian construction, tensor representations, and hybrid optimization experiments using QAOA, VQE, or quantum annealing.

Evaluation approach

Time-ordered backtesting, classical optimization baselines, transaction-cost assumptions, and out-of-sample risk measures.

Explore platform components +
  • Market Hamiltonian constructor from time-series data
  • Market State Tensor with streaming-update research
  • Portfolio optimization with QAOA, VQE, and annealing
  • Regime classification: bull, bear, crisis, recovery
  • Cross-asset correlation tensor construction
  • Tail-risk estimation and scenario analysis
  • Backtesting against classical quant methods
  • Q-MEHR integration research for market analytics

Research integration directions: Python / R workflows, cloud APIs, on-premise compute, and quantum annealing backends.

Components and interfaces describe the research architecture; availability is discussed for each collaboration.
