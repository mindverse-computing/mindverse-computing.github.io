QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

Quantum Virtual Finance

QVF

Portfolio and risk teams need earlier signals than classical factors can provide. QVF tracks evolving market state health with quantum-classical simulation, then uses agentic workflows to deliver regime warnings and actionable controls before instability is fully visible in traditional dashboards.

Problem We Solve

Regime breaks are nonlinear and often missed until after drawdown. QVF models market dynamics with adaptive state analytics, detects transitions earlier, and lets agentic workflows recommend portfolio rebalancing with risk-aware control logic.

Use Cases & Applications

  • Market state reconstruction and continuity tracking
  • Instability and recovery early warning
  • Regime tagging for market condition awareness
  • Transition monitoring for timely risk response
  • Auditable market-state history generation
  • Cross-horizon consistency checks
  • Stress testing for resilient strategy design
  • Agentic guided portfolio rebalancing workflows

QUANTUM CHEMISTRY & MATERIALS SCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Matter

QVMa

Materials scientists face challenges predicting novel material properties, catalyst performance, and chemical reaction pathways. QVMa provides accurate quantum-classical simulations for battery materials, catalysts, and semiconductors, then uses agentic experiment-design workflows to reduce experimental R&D costs and accelerating discovery timelines from years to weeks.

Problem We Solve

Classical computational chemistry methods are too slow and inaccurate for complex molecular systems. QVMa delivers quantum-accurate simulations of molecular energies, reaction mechanisms, and material properties, with agentic candidate ranking at speeds and scales that transform the materials discovery process—enabling rational design of next-generation energy storage, catalysts, and semiconductors.

Use Cases & Applications

  • Battery materials: lithium-ion, solid-state electrolyte design
  • Catalyst screening for green chemistry & carbon capture
  • Semiconductor band gap engineering for photovoltaics
  • Drug molecule quantum property prediction
  • Superconductor discovery & high-temperature materials
  • Polymer design for advanced manufacturing
  • Reaction pathway optimization for chemical synthesis
  • Phase transition modeling for alloy development

GENOMICS & OMICS

Quantum Virtual Omics

QVO

QVO translates multi-omics measurements into executable disease physics. Teams can move from static biomarkers to perturbation-aware models that explain disease drift, then prioritize interventions that guide systems back toward healthier behavior.

Problem We Solve

Most discovery stacks do not preserve regulatory topology across omics layers. QVO encodes patient state as a dynamic baseline, quantifies defect class, and searches for therapy options that reverse pathological drift with explicit mechanistic traceability.

Use Cases & Applications

  • Patient-level state record construction, replay, and agentic triage
  • Disease perturbation fingerprinting
  • Therapy search and ranking
  • Tissue landscape simulation
  • Defect taxonomy triage
  • Combination strategy design for oncology programs
  • Mechanistic cohort stratification for trials
  • Rare disease pathway stabilization studies

BCI & NEUROSCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Mind

QVM

QVM converts live BCI streams into an agentic cognitive twin that tracks system-state topology in real time. This supports continuous state estimation, longitudinal cognitive baselining, and recommendation-guided closed-loop intervention design beyond static classifier outputs.

Problem We Solve

Current BCI models underrepresent coherent and multistable neural dynamics. QVM learns adaptive coupling structure and writes a longitudinal state record for cognitive hypotheses, anomaly triage, and intervention planning.

Use Cases & Applications

  • Neural phasor mapping for EEG/ECoG pipelines
  • Cognitive condition monitoring across behavioral states
  • Cognitive state stability scoring
  • Closed-loop stimulation policy prototyping
  • Longitudinal timelines for cohort studies
  • Motor-intent interface calibration workflows
  • Agentic neurofeedback protocols driven by state drift
  • Endpoint design for neuromodulation trials
