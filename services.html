What we are proposing to build on the platform — for learning and for neurological research — and an honest account of how far each idea has got.

What we are proposing to build

Our proposed research runs in two directions from a single state model: how healthy brains learn, and how conditions move a brain away from its own baseline.

Read the status labels

Almost everything below is a proposal. The state model is built and benchmarked; the applications are research directions we have specified and are now developing.

HEALTHY DIRECTION

Superlearning — Proposed research

Today’s adaptive learning tools watch answers. That tells you what a student has already failed to learn — too late, and it cannot distinguish a learner who is stretched from one who stopped absorbing twenty minutes ago.

Reading state directly could. Whether it does is the research question, not a claim.

What we would need to show

That a state model built from a learner’s own recordings predicts readiness better than their answer history does. No such study has been run.

Research Products

  • Superlearning Mind Atlas Proposed

    A map of healthy variation in the dynamics a BCI can see — between people, and within one person as they learn. A learner would be a position on the map; learning a path through it.

  • Learning Track Designer Proposed

    Would turn a learner’s position into a sequence: what to study next, at what difficulty, and when to stop for the day. Designed to sit behind an existing platform.

  • Cortical State Library Proposed

    A reference catalogue of distinct states across arousal, sensorimotor, cognitive and affective tiers. Specified and scheduled.

CLINICAL DIRECTION

Neurological Perturbation Studies

Neurological conditions unfold over months and years, but the measurements taken along the way sit in unconnected documents.

Our proposed clinical research studies how a condition moves a person away from their own healthy baseline — measured against themselves rather than a population average.

Not a medical device

Nothing in this programme diagnoses, prescribes, or is used in patient care. Any future clinical application would require regulatory approval, ethical review, and clinical validation. No patient data has been fitted.

Research Products

  • Longitudinal Neural State Record Proposed

    Would hold a quantitative model of the person’s healthy brain function alongside how far their condition has moved them from it, tracking change rather than storing a diagnosis code.

  • Neural Perturbation Atlas Proposed

    A reference library of neurological and psychiatric conditions, each characterised by its signature in the recorded dynamics. Specified in draft.

  • Closed-Loop Neuromodulation Research Proposed

    Would read live signals, compare against the healthy reference, and rank candidate stimulation approaches for researchers to evaluate — never an instruction to administer.

What comes next

These are early in-house results and an encouraging signal. Our approach is not better at everything — conventional methods still lead on short-horizon prediction, and quantum speed-up is not part of our claim. Larger cohorts and independent replication are the next milestones, and that work is underway.
