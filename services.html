QUANTITATIVE FINANCE

Quantum Virtual Finance — Research

QVF

Our finance research develops next-generation tools for understanding how markets move, how risk concentrates, and how portfolios can be managed with greater intelligence. These tools are built on our QVF platform and target systematic trading, risk management, and market intelligence.

What We're Building

We are developing market connectivity mapping tools, automated trading signal systems, and portfolio optimisation methods that detect regime changes faster than conventional approaches — and a monthly market intelligence report for institutional investors.

Research Products

  • Finatom A network-based representation of global market connectivity that maps how assets, sectors, and macro signals are dynamically coupled. Continuously updated as market conditions evolve, it surfaces regime transitions and risk concentrations before they appear in conventional price indicators. In Development
  • Quantum Portfolio Optimisation (QPO) A next-generation portfolio allocation engine that goes beyond classical mean-variance methods. QPO finds risk-return balanced portfolios under stressed, non-linear market conditions — capturing tail dependencies and correlation breakdowns that conventional optimisers overlook. In Development
  • Quantum High-Frequency Trading (QHFT) An ultra-low-latency market state tracking system that detects micro-structural transitions in real time. QHFT generates execution signals at the precise moment market structure begins to shift — enabling positioning ahead of moves that slower models miss entirely. In Development
  • Market State Report (MSR) A monthly quantitative briefing for institutional investors and systematic funds. Each edition covers market connectivity maps, sector coupling heatmaps, volatility regime assessments, and risk-adjusted signal rankings — all through the QVF lens. Coming Soon

QUANTUM CHEMISTRY & MATERIALS SCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Matter — Research

QVMa

Our materials research builds simulation tools that predict how molecules and materials behave at the atomic level — going beyond what classical chemistry software can model. We are creating databases, design frameworks, and planning tools for chemistry, energy, and drug discovery.

What We're Building

We are building a searchable database of chemical reactions, a framework for designing complex molecular systems layer by layer, and an intelligent tool that proposes how to synthesise a target material from first principles.

Research Products

  • ChemSphere A searchable, graph-structured database of chemical reactions built for quantum chemistry research. Researchers can query reaction pathways, compare molecular fingerprints across chemical space, identify known synthesis routes, and profile reaction intermediates and transition states — all within a single connected knowledge base. In Development
  • Hierarchical Agentic Hamiltonian Design (HAHD) A multi-agent design framework for building complex molecular and material systems from the ground up. HAHD decomposes a target into interacting layers — electronic structure, atomic geometry, vibrational modes, and environmental coupling — each managed by a specialist AI agent. The agents collaborate to produce an accurate, composite simulation of the full system, enabling design of materials and drug candidates at scales beyond classical tools. In Development
  • Synthesis Planner An intelligent synthesis planning tool powered by HAHD simulations and the ChemSphere reaction database. Given a target material or molecule, it proposes which precursors, reaction conditions, and synthesis steps are most likely to succeed — then ranks each candidate route by predicted yield and confidence, dramatically compressing the time from idea to validated synthesis path. Coming Soon

GENOMICS & CELL BIOLOGY

Quantum Virtual Omics — Research

QVO

Our omics research translates complex biological data — from genes and proteins to metabolites and clinical signals — into dynamic models of how cells and patients function. These tools support drug discovery, personalised medicine, and the design of smarter clinical trials.

What We're Building

We are building tools that model biological state from multi-omics data, identify the molecular signatures of disease, rank potential therapeutic interventions by their predicted recovery effect, and create a new kind of patient health record that goes beyond static diagnostic codes.

Research Products

  • Omics-HAHD Applies the Hierarchical Agentic Hamiltonian Design (HAHD) framework to multi-omics biology — integrating genomic, transcriptomic, proteomic, and metabolomic layers into a unified model of biological state. Each molecular layer is handled by a specialist agent, enabling the system to identify which biological disruptions drive disease, and which molecular targets are most likely to restore healthy cellular function. In Development
  • Perturbation & Inversion Analysis A systematic method for identifying what is going wrong inside a diseased cell and what can fix it. The engine classifies the type of biological disruption, then works backwards to find which interventions — small molecules, gene edits, or biologics — are most likely to reverse it. Candidates are ranked by their predicted ability to restore the healthy biological state, with full mechanistic reasoning. In Development
  • Quantum Electronic Health Record (Q-EHR) A new kind of patient record that replaces static diagnosis codes with a quantitative model of how a patient's biology is actually functioning. Q-EHR tracks changes in biological state over time — under disease, treatment, and recovery — enabling personalised monitoring and more precise clinical decisions than today's ICD-based systems allow. Research Preview
  • Cell State Atlas A reference library of biological states built from large-scale single-cell datasets across tissues, disease conditions, and treatment contexts. Researchers use the atlas to compare a patient's biological state against known healthy and disease references, predict how they will respond to a given drug, and identify biomarkers for clinical stratification. Coming Soon

BCI & NEUROSCIENCE

Quantum Virtual Mind — Research

QVM

Our neuroscience research builds tools for understanding brain state, detecting neurological conditions early, and designing targeted brain stimulation therapies. These tools are for researchers, clinicians, and companies building the next generation of brain-computer interfaces.

What We're Building

We are building a reference atlas of neurological conditions with their brain-state signatures, a new kind of neural health record, an autonomous closed-loop brain stimulation system, and a library of brain states for EEG-based decoding.

Research Products

  • Neural Perturbation Atlas A comprehensive reference library covering a wide range of neurological and psychiatric conditions — including epilepsy, depression, Parkinson's disease, schizophrenia, ADHD, TBI, and Alzheimer's — each characterised by its specific brain-state signature. The atlas enables early detection, comparison of a patient's state against known profiles, and prediction of which brain stimulation approach is most likely to restore healthy function. In Development
  • Quantum Neural Electronic Health Record (Q-NEHR) A dynamic patient record that captures the full state of a patient's brain health — not just a list of symptoms or diagnosis codes. Q-NEHR stores a quantitative model of healthy brain function for each patient, together with how that state has shifted under their specific condition. It tracks cognitive changes over time, supports personalised therapy planning, and provides measurable biomarkers for mental health and neurological care. Research Preview
  • Closed-Loop Brain Stimulation An autonomous therapy system that operates continuously: it reads live brain signals, builds a real-time model of the patient's current brain state, compares it against their healthy reference, selects the optimal stimulation approach from a library of candidate protocols (tDCS, tACS, TMS, or DBS), applies the chosen stimulation, and then measures how much the brain state has moved toward recovery. The loop runs adaptively — tightening or shifting its parameters as the patient responds. In Development
  • Cortical State Library A reference catalogue of distinct brain states organised across functional tiers: arousal and baseline (deep sleep, REM, meditation), sensorimotor (motor imagery, visual evoked responses), cognitive (working memory, language, attention), affective and social (fear, reward, empathy), and pathological (seizure, Parkinson's tremor, depression, coma). Researchers and BCI engineers use the library to classify EEG signals, design neural decoders, and benchmark brain-computer interface systems. Coming Soon
