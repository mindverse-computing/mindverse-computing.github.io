Fit criteria

A useful application
starts with a reference.

Prioritize questions with available inputs, a defined comparison, and an outcome that can be evaluated.

Defined system

Specify geometry, operating regime, cohort, recording task, or market universe.

Usable evidence

Document data provenance, preparation, access, and a meaningful reference method.

Bounded objective

State the quantity to predict or optimize and the conditions under which it would be useful.
